📝 Description

Jász Stones: We are the Jászberény team of the Csányi Foundation. We have been competing for 5 years, and we created our project for the First LEGO League competition.

This year's season: Connected to the "Unearthed" theme, we chose the Jász Horn, an important symbol of Jász identity.

Goal: The goal of the project is to make the Jász Horn experienceable for everyone – especially for visually impaired people and people with disabilities – because the original artifact cannot be touched in the museum.
